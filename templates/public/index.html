Enrollment Process

  1. Create an account and verify your email address.
  2. Fill out the application form and choose your program.
  3. Upload your requirements for review by the registrar.
  4. Follow your schedule for the exam, interview and medical.
  5. Submit your original documents to the school for evaluation and payment of fees.
  6. Receive your student number and your institutional email account.

About SIT

“Faithful in living, faithful in teaching”

SIMONS Institute of Theology is the theological training arm of New Testament Baptist Church. It is tasked to train church leaders and workers for the work of ministry, and seeks to produce pastors and missionaries for local and international ministries.

Vision

SIMONS Institute of Theology shall be a leading theological institute in modeling Christ's life and teaching.

Mission

SIMONS Institute of Theology shall achieve its vision through Christian life practical training, in-depth study of Christian doctrines, and constant Christian discipline.

Core Values

  • Deeds: Life that is steered by the Spirit of Christ (Gal. 5:22-26).
  • Doctrine: Life that is sanctified by the truth of Christ (Jn. 17:17).
  • Discipline: Life that is surrendered in obedience to Christ (Lk. 9:23).
